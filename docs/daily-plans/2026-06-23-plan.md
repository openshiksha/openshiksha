## B. Why this matters

- **Lock in the gain we already paid for.** A11Y-6's whole point was to *gate* the
  student loop; leaving it `gate: false` means any future PR can silently
  reintroduce a label/contrast regression on the surfaces students live on, with
  CI staying green. A11Y-8 is a tiny, near-zero-risk PR that converts measured-clean
  into regression-proof — the cheapest high-value item on the board.
- **Teachers and parents are gatekeepers.** A teacher who can't author or grade
  with a keyboard/screen reader, or a low-vision parent who can't read the insights
  dashboard, blocks an entire classroom/family from the platform. Batch 3 extends
  the measured-and-gated AA contract from the student front door to the people who
  bring students in. That is DoD item 6.
- **The harness is already built — this is activation, not invention.** A11Y-6
  stood up `e2e/support/auth.ts` (`setupStudentAuth` + `page.route` API mocks) and
  the parameterized `auth?: boolean` route table. Batch 3 is mostly *new fixtures
  for two more roles* riding that exact pattern. Low-risk, compounding, mostly
  frontend/test infra; no backend schema change.
- **Honest about scope.** The student loop was unusually clean. The dense teacher
  authoring/grading surfaces (`CreateQuestionPage`, grading queue) likely *do* carry
  real label/landmark findings — that's expected and is precisely what A11Y-11
  remediates. Any surface that needs more than a mechanical fix stays reporting-mode
  (not gated) and is noted, per the Batch-1 lesson — we never red-wall CI on a dirty
  route.
