# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in OpenShiksha a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

OpenShiksha exists to serve students and teachers, and our community should
reflect the same care.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and welcoming, especially to newcomers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainers at **conduct@openshiksha.org**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions or comments that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban
contributors for serious or repeated violations.
